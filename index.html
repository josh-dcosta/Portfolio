About Me

Joshua Dcosta

Hello, my name is Josh Dcosta. I'm a freshman studying Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I'm taking this course because I want to deepen my knowledge about AI systems by getting a thorough understanding of their history and how they work.

I hope to get enough knowledge out of this course to the point where I can start designing my own AI models on various things that I'm passionate about. This could be anything from Sports Analytics to the Healthcare Field.

Continuing, one specific moment where AI/ML showed up in my life was when I was at an engineering fair and saw a medical model being used to help diagnose lung cancer.

Although AI is quite powerful, I do have my concerns about it. The biggest one being that AI will go rogue and start hacking into various confidential databases and security networks, compromising sensitive information about others.

As for the course objectives that I'm most curious about, I would say the key moments in the historical development of AI and ML, and how they relate to modern AI systems. This is meaningful to me because I have always been fascinated with the history of AI and seeing how it has developed is something that I find very interesting.

Finally, one detail about me outside of this class is that I love to watch the Eagles.
