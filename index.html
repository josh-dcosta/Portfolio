Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Explain key moments in the historical development of AI/ML and relate them to modern AI systems.

Evidence

One outside source that helped me better understand the historical development of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning was a Stanford University article titled A Short History of AI. The article explains how AI developed from early ideas about machine intelligence to the advanced systems we use today. One important moment in AI history occurred in 1950, when Alan Turing published his paper called Computing Machinery and Intelligence. In this paper, Turing explored whether machines show intelligent behavior and introduced what became known as the Turing Test. This was important because it encouraged researchers to think about whether computers could do tasks that would normally need human intelligence. Continuing, another major milestone took place in 1956 at the Dartmouth workshop. Here, the term Artificial Intelligence was introduced, and AI became an established field of research.

Another development that I found to be interesting was Arthur Samuel's checkers program. During the 1950s, Samuel made a program that could improve its performance by playing checkers and learning from experience. This was an important early example of machine learning since it showed that computers could improve a task without needing every move to be explicitly programmed by a human. This concept still applies today because modern Machine Learning systems learn patterns from data and use them to make predictions or decisions. For example, recommendation systems and fraud detection both use Machine Learning techniques to identify patterns and make good decisions.

I also found it interesting how early research into Artificial Neural Networks led to modern Deep Learning. Today, Neural Networks are used in tech like facial recognition, medical image analysis, and speech recognition. Seeing this connection helped me understand that modern AI systems are built upon ideas that researchers have been developing for decades. Overall, this article showed me that AI didn't become advanced overnight. Instead, it developed through a lot of years of research and improvements in computing technology. As someone studying Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, I find it cool to see how early concepts have developed into the technologies we use today. It makes me more interested in learning about the future of AI and how I could contribute to its development, especially in areas such as healthcare and sports analytics.

Identify and discuss ethical issues associated with AI and ML and their societal implications.

Evidence

Throughout CS 180, I have been able to learn that AI and ML can be powerful tools for solving real-world problems. However, they can also have potential ethical concerns that can impact society. One assignment that helped me better understand this was Homework 3. Here, I analyzed two case studies that involved predictive policing and protein folding. In the first case study, an AI system called Safe Zone used historical crime data to predict criminal activity and assign police patrols. The issue in this case was that since the historical data reflected existing inequalities, the system disproportionately targeted minority neighborhoods and reinforced biases in policing. This supports the idea that AI systems are not necessarily objective just because they rely on data, and that biased information can lead to unfair outcomes. In the other study, an AI model called ProteoGen was developed to predict protein structures and support medical research. Although the technology had the potential to improve healthcare, it highlighted concerns about dual-use technology, a big topic in AI ethics. Additionally, unequal access to computing resources brought questions about fairness in scientific research. Overall, these case studies helped understand the importance of algorithmic fairness, transparency, and responsible AI development. Going forward, I think that developers should consider not only what AI systems are capable of doing, but also how those systems could affect different communities and society.

One source that demonstrates the ethical issues associated with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning was UNESCO's article, "Working for, Not Against Humanity." This article discusses how the quick development of AI creates important ethical questions about its impact on individuals and society. One issue that stood out to me was the potential loss of privacy. As AI systems become more advanced, they are more capable of collecting, processing, and analyzing large amounts of personal information. Even though this can make certain technologies more useful and efficient, it also creates concerns about how personal data is stored and used.

Continuing, another important issue discussed in the article is the possibility that AI could reinforce social inequalities that already exist. AI systems are developed using information and decisions made by humans. This means that the values and biases of their developers can influence how these systems work. For example, if an AI system is trained using biased information, it could come up with unfair results when making decisions about individuals. This is concerning because people might trust computer-generated decisions without questioning if they are fair. The article also raises some questions about accountability and who should be responsible when AI systems negatively affect people. I think this is especially important as AI becomes more common in areas like healthcare and employment. Here, its decisions can have significant consequences.

One point from the article that I found interesting was the question of whose ethical values should guide AI development. Different people and communities have different beliefs about what is fair, which makes it important to consider a variety of perspectives when creating these technologies. This article helped me understand that the development of AI involves more than improving technology and making systems more efficient. Developers have to consider privacy, fairness, and the potential consequences their systems could have on society. Ultimately, I think responsible development is important to ensure that AI benefits people rather than creating more problems.

Describe how core AI techniques such as search and adversarial reasoning are used to model intelligent behavior.

Evidence

One real-world application of search and adversarial reasoning in AI is chess engines like Stockfish. The MIT Press article, "How a Computer Plays Chess," explains how chess programs use algorithms to analyze possible moves and choose the best decisions. Chess is a good example of intelligent problem-solving because an AI system has to consider different possibilities while anticipating what its opponent might do next. Rather than selecting moves randomly, the engine evaluates possible outcomes before it makes a decision.

One important technique talked about in the article is the minimax algorithm. This allows an AI to search through possible moves and predict an opponent's responses. The algorithm organizes possible moves into a search tree, where each branch represents a different move and the resulting board position. The AI evaluates these positions and tries to make the move that gives it the best outcome, assuming its opponent will also make the strongest possible move. This is an example of adversarial reasoning because the system has to account for another player's decisions when planning its own strategy. The article also talks about pruning. This allows chess engines to avoid exploring certain branches that are not likely to affect their final decision. This makes the search process more efficient by reducing the number of possibilities the engine needs to look at.

I found this application interesting because it shows how multiple AI techniques work together to model intelligent behavior. Search allows a chess engine to look at possible moves and future positions, while adversarial reasoning helps it anticipate an opponent's responses. Together, these techniques allow AI systems to plan ahead and make strategic decisions. This shows how AI can approach complex problems by evaluating possible outcomes instead of simply reacting to the current situation.
